The lane

The industry is arguing about AI in the worst possible way.

One side says adapt or die. The other says it is all theft, do not touch it. Both are loud, both are exhausting, and neither one helps you when you sit down to work tomorrow morning.

SanTor Media Group does the practical version instead: which tools are actually worth your time, how to get your hours back from the busywork, and how to protect the part of the work that is irreplaceably yours. Craft is the constant. Everything else is just tools.
